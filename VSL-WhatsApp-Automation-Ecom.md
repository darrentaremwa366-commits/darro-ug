1. **Speak Ugandan business English.** Say "shillings" not "UGX" when speaking (write UGX on screen). Say "WhatsApp" not "message app."
2. **The money framing is your strongest opener.** "More sales from the customers who already message you" — repeat this phrase at least 3 times in the video.
3. **Show the REAL simulator conversations.** The upsell playbook (Joshua buying the Portugal jersey after Argentina) is your strongest "wow" moment. Pause on the "AOV +85%" system message.
4. **Show the dashboard on mobile if possible.** Your audience watches on phones. Show them the conversations page on a phone screen.
5. **Don't over-explain features.** This is a VSL, not a tutorial. Show the result — "this message was sent automatically 2 days later" — not the settings.
6. **When you say "I'm a student in China, my customers are in Uganda" — slow down.** That's the credibility moment. It explains WHY automation matters (time zone difference).
7. **Record in two browser tabs.** One with the simulator, one with the admin conversations dashboard. Switch smoothly.
8. **Mention "Shopify" once early.** Around the intro — "modeled on how Shopify stores convert." One time is enough.
9. **End with the refrain.** "More sales from the traffic you already have." Hold eye contact, then fade.
