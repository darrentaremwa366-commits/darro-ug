## PART 2: THE AUTOMATION (0:55 - 1:35)

**SCREEN:** Switch browser tab. Open /simulator.

**WHAT TO SHOW:**
1. /simulator — show the playbook selection screen (4 playbooks listed)
2. Click "Speed to Lead" — press play, show the first 2 messages:
   - Order intake: "Thanks for your order! Where should we deliver?"

3. Stop it. Click "Cart Reactivation" — press play, show the first 2 messages:
   - "You left something behind" with the product name
   - Member price offer
4. Quick mention of the other two: "Drop Announcement" and "Delivery Confirmation"

**TALKING POINTS:**
- Second: the automation. This is what runs while you sleep
- Four automated flows. I will show you two
- Speed to Lead: a customer orders at 2 AM. The system confirms the order within minutes, collects delivery location, and notifies the dashboard. You wake up to confirmed orders
- Cart Reactivation: a customer added a jersey to their cart and went quiet. Two days later, the system sends a message with the product they were looking at, offers the member price, and if they still do not respond, sends a discount code
- I did not type any of these messages. The system sent them on its own
- The other two: Drop Announcement messages past buyers when new stock arrives. Delivery Confirmation sends updates from shipment to doorstep

**KEY LINE:** "Customers who would have disappeared are recovered automatically. You do nothing."

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** Recovers lost sales, confirms orders 24/7, sells to past buyers without new marketing.

## PART 5: THE NUMBERS (2:45 - 3:20)

**SCREEN:** Open /admin/analytics, then switch to /admin/customers.

**WHAT TO SHOW:**
1. /admin/analytics — show the conversion funnel:
   - Product Views at the top
   - Add to Carts
   - Checkout Starts
   - Purchases at the bottom
   - Drop rates between each step
2. Point at the biggest drop — "This is where you are losing the most people"
3. Scroll to Top 10 Products
4. Switch to /admin/customers — show:
   - New Customers vs Returning
   - Repeat Purchase Rate
   - Lifetime Value per customer
   - Median time to second order

**TALKING POINTS:**
- Last: the numbers that tell you where to fix
- This funnel shows exactly where customers disappear. If 100 people view your products but only 3 buy — where are the 97 going?
- The biggest drop between steps tells you what to fix first. If they leave between product view and add-to-cart, your product pages need work. If they leave between checkout start and purchase, your checkout needs work
- On the customers page: how many are new, how many are coming back, what is each customer worth over their lifetime, and how long it takes for them to buy a second time
- This is the difference between guessing and knowing

**KEY LINE:** "If you do not know where customers are dropping off, you are guessing at the fix. This tells you exactly where."

**WHY THIS MATTERS:** Targeted fixes based on data. Know which products to push, which traffic source produces buyers, and whether past customers are coming back.

## RECORDING CHECKLIST

- [ ] Make sure /simulator is loaded and ready to play
- [ ] Close unnecessary tabs so the browser runs smooth
- [ ] Test screen recording — make sure both tabs are visible
- [ ] Have your face cam positioned in the bottom-right corner

**Recording flow (one take, scroll through):**
1. Start with face cam — deliver the hook
2. Switch to Tab 1 — /shop, walk through to /cart to WhatsApp button
3. Stay in Tab 1 — switch to /simulator, play Speed to Lead + Cart Reactivation
4. Switch to Tab 2 — /admin/overview, scroll through KPIs
5. Stay in Tab 2 — /admin/conversations, show list + detail
6. Stay in Tab 2 — /admin/analytics, show funnel, then /admin/customers
7. Switch to face cam — deliver the money summary + CTA

**Pacing notes:**
- Do not rush the dashboard scroll — the KPI cards are dense, viewers need time to read
- When playing the simulator, slow it down if your platform has a speed control
- Speak in business-owner language: "customers," "orders," "sales," "recover" — never "API," "playbook," "webhook"
- The transition between parts should be quick — "Second: the automation" — do not pause
- Keep the tone calm and analytical, not hyped
