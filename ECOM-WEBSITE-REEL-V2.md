# E-COMMERCE REEL SCRIPT — V2

**Product:** Website (product catalog + WhatsApp ordering)
**Format:** Instagram Reels / WhatsApp Status
**Length:** 2:30
**Language:** Simple English — understandable to a 12-year-old
**Pricing in video:** NONE. **CTA in video:** NONE. Both live in the caption (Phase 3 below).

**V2 changes from V1:**
- Hook now speaks directly to the person (product business owner), tells them what they're about to see, and states the problem — before any big brand appears
- Website demo starts at ~0:17 (V1 was 0:40) — as fast as possible
- Big brand proof (Jumia, Gymshark/Shopify) is no longer the opening — it is woven INTO the feature walkthrough
- Thesis line added: marketing gets customers, a system converts them — that's what big brands have and most African businesses don't

---

## THE BELIEF WE ARE BREAKING

> "Instagram is enough — my customers already find me there."

**Truth the video delivers:** Marketing is not the problem. Conversion is. Instagram cannot show everything, cannot sell at 2am, and cannot follow up. Big brands run a system. That is why they convert.

---

## THE HOOK FORMULA (used in this and every niche script)

1. **Call out the person** — name the niche + where they sell
2. **Tell them what they're about to see** — the demo walkthrough + the outcome
3. **State the problem** — most businesses (African) focus on marketing, not conversion. Big brands have a system.
4. **GO** — straight to the website. No brand intro, no history.

For any other niche script, only line 1 changes ("If you run a restaurant..." / "If you own a hotel...").

## PHASE 3 — CAPTION + CTA (unchanged from V1)

### 1. Instagram caption

> Built this demo for product brand owners here in Uganda.
>
> Running a free 14-day pilot — you use the full site with your own products, share it with your customers, and only pay if it actually brings you orders. Taking 5 this month.
>
> DM "PILOT".

### 2. WhatsApp status version (shorter)

> Free 14-day pilot for one product brand — full website, products, prices, and WhatsApp orders. You share it with customers and see what happens. Only pay if it works. 5 slots this month. Reply PILOT.

### 3. DM reply templates

**Template 1 — Qualification (first reply when someone sends "PILOT")**

> Great — before I give you the pilot slot, three quick questions so I know it will actually work for you:
>
> 1. What do you sell, and are you selling it already? (clothing, beauty, accessories, home goods, etc.)
> 2. Where do you sell now — Instagram, WhatsApp, a shop, or all of them?
> 3. Roughly how many "how much is this?" messages do you get in a day, and how many of those actually buy?
>
> If you already have customers and products, you are a fit. If you are just starting, this is not for you yet — I would rather tell you now than waste your two weeks.

**Template 2 — Demo walkthrough (once they qualify)**

> Perfect. Here is the demo so you can see exactly what you would get:
> https://darro-ug.vercel.app/
>
> Look at three things while you are there:
> 1. The shop page — every jersey with its price and size, so nobody has to ask you.
> 2. Add something to the cart, then tap the WhatsApp button — see how the order arrives already written out for you.
> 3. On a sold-out jersey, look at what happens to that customer. That is a customer you currently lose.
>
> Tell me which of those three you feel most — that is usually the one costing you the most money.

**Template 3 — Pilot start steps**

> Here is how the pilot works:
>
> 1. You send me 8–15 of your products — photos, names, prices, sizes.
> 2. I build your store with your brand, your products, your prices. About 48 hours.
> 3. You get the link and share it with your customers wherever you already sell.
> 4. We run it for 14 days. You keep every order that comes in.
> 5. At the end you decide. If it brought you orders and you want to keep it, we continue. If not, you walk away and you owe nothing.
>
> The only thing I ask: you actually share the link with your customers. A store nobody visits cannot prove anything. Ready to send me your products?
